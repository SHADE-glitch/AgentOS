# Skill Quality Metrics

## Objective

Measure whether individual skills are performing well and whether they are creating unnecessary conflicts.

## Metrics

```text
Skill:
Usage Frequency:
Success Rate:
Failure Frequency:
Conflict Frequency:
Quality Score:
Recommendation:
```

## Example

```text
Skill: rag-engineer
Usage Frequency: 18
Success Rate: 94%
Failure Frequency: 1
Conflict Frequency: 1
Quality Score: 0.91
Recommendation: stable; retain and monitor with RAG benchmark coverage
```
